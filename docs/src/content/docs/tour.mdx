Encore is set up and run from its web interface. These screenshots follow your light or dark theme; addresses, Amazon IDs and similar details are blurred.

## Overview

The dashboard: the skill's state, whether Alexa can reach Encore, each Kodi instance and what it's playing, today's requests, and anything that needs you.

## Kodi

Add Kodi instances, found on your network or entered by address, test them, sync their libraries and see shared libraries. See [Kodi instances](/encore/guides/kodi/).

## Echo devices

Echos appear after their first request; link each to the Kodi instance it controls. See [Echo devices and rooms](/encore/guides/echo-devices/).

## On a phone

The same pages fit a phone, and Encore can be [installed as an app](/encore/getting-started/first-run/#install-as-an-app). The sections move to a menu bar along the bottom with four of them; **More** holds the rest, the theme and **Sign out**. Tables show each row as a card, and a request in the log opens under its row.

### Arrange the menu

Open **More**, then **Arrange menu**, to choose which four sections sit in the menu bar and in what order. Drag a section by its handle or use its arrows; the minus and plus buttons move a section between the menu bar and More. **Reset to default** puts the original four back. The arrangement is saved on that device, so a phone and a tablet can differ.
